# FABLE_GAUNTLET_COMPARISON

## Package level (dry-run, byte-verifiable)

`diff -r fixtures/engine/snapshots/gauntlet-before gauntlet-after`.
Before: `generate_beats` = 4 thin rules; `generate_scene` = zero craft
rules and a cast block with NO relationship data (verified in the
artifact — the five-person Second Wedding web reached the model as two
prose sentences); `sync_story_to_script` = format rules only. After:
causality/escalation/obvious-version-ban/final-beat contracts (beats);
purpose-as-contract, decision-or-revelation, subtext pair, swap-test
voices, stock-gesture class ban, 100–400 words (scene); compounding +
continuity-of-knowledge + voice stability (script); shared
`BIBLE_FIDELITY_RULES`; and `Relationships (FIXED FACTS)` lines in the
bible + scene cast block.

## Output level (live, scored)

**The fidelity trap, before vs after.** Before-state evidence: Phase 3
produced "his creditor's daughter" twice on Buck Mark, and the audit
proved relationships never reached the model. After: on the *maximal*
trap (Second Wedding — half-sister bride, asymmetric stepfather, a
former stepdaughter who is nobody's relative at the wedding), one
scene + a four-scene script produced **zero relationship errors** and
one bullseye that could only come from the structured data:

> GENE: And now the maid of honor. My almost-granddaughter. Sadie.

"Almost-granddaughter" is exactly the trap's asymmetry (Gene is
stepfather to Ruth only; Sadie is Ruth's FORMER stepdaughter) —
inferable from no single prose sentence in the fixture.

**Scene intent, before vs after.** The before-prompt carried a beat's
purpose as an optional context line. After, purpose is "THIS IS THE
SCENE'S CONTRACT" — and the hardest fixture (reveal must land through
a screen while the voice keeps talking, twist 9, snippet to weave)
scored 16/16:

> The dialog box clears itself. Behind it, account #40-1187 is grayed
> out. A red banner stamps across Louise Prewitt's name:
> **ACCOUNT CLOSED — ACCT HOLDER DECEASED 03/14**
> Mac is still facing the window.
> LOUISE (V.O.): Mackenzie? You still there, honey?

**Structure, before vs after.** The before-ask permitted "and then"
lists and template sheets. After, on the fixture DESIGNED to elicit
the haunted-house template (unpredictability 8, helpful-horror
premise): zero template beats, escalation that changes in kind
(intrusion → complicity), and a closing image in the premise's own
vocabulary ("Theo locks the front door from inside"). On the
Breaking-Bad-adjacent fixture: zero plot furniture; the descent runs
on fentanyl patches, waste margins, and audit ledgers.

**Cohesion.** The four-scene script compounds across cuts (the
settlement file planted in scene 1's drawer detonates in scene 4's
"Ruth wrote the vows. Ruth filed the divorce."; the empty chair
travels from the seating-chart scene to the toast). Voices hold:
Sadie's timestamped-deposition style is present in every Sadie line.

## Honest limits

- One-sample-per-cell: each fixture×surface ran once (plus one
  diagnosed retry). The pattern is consistent across 11 calls, but
  variance isn't characterized.
- Horror-comedy's comedy register came out thinner than its toneNote
  demands (the one partially-confirmed prediction).
- Findings F2–F4 (empty relationship arrays in real projects, markdown
  artifacts, climax length overrun) are logged, not fixed.
- Before/after is package-vs-package plus scored after-outputs; no
  live "before" baseline existed for beats/scene/script (nothing had
  ever been measured), so the before-state rests on the audit's
  documented absences + Phase-3's live garbles.
